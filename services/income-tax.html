Income Tax Registration & E-filing

Assistance with relevant income-tax registration requirements, return preparation and electronic filing, depending on your circumstances and applicable rules.

Service overview

Understand the work, information and next steps.

Assistance with income tax return preparation, filing and related tax matters, based on the information and documents you provide.

What we can assist with

  • Income tax filing and prompt e-filing for all taxpayer categories (ITR-1 through ITR-7).
  • Income tax return preparation, income computation and deduction optimization.
  • Proactive tax planning and advance tax estimation.
  • Tax preparation, financial records reconciliation, and AIS/TIS/26AS verification.
  • Guidance on relevant income-tax registration requirements, where applicable.
  • Review of the information and records needed for the relevant filing.

Who may need this service?

Salaried individuals, professionals, freelancers, proprietors and other taxpayers where the service is applicable.

How the process may work

  1. Understand your income sources and filing requirements.
  2. Review the relevant information and supporting documents.
  3. Prepare the return based on verified details and applicable rules.
  4. Confirm the details with you before the appropriate filing step.
The exact requirements depend on your circumstances and applicable law. The firm should confirm eligibility, scope, fees and timelines before work begins.
