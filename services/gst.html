Service overview

Understand the work, information and next steps.

Practical assistance with applicable GST registrations, returns and recurring compliance requirements for businesses.

Who may need this service?

Eligible businesses, proprietors and other persons who need assistance understanding or meeting GST obligations.

GST services available

Support can be discussed for the following services, subject to the taxpayer’s eligibility, records and applicable rules.

  • GST Registration: assistance with registration where required and applicable.
  • GST Monthly Returns: preparation and filing support for applicable monthly returns.
  • GST Annual Return (GSTR-9): assistance where the annual return is applicable to the taxpayer.

How the process may work

  1. Understand the business activity and current GST status.
  2. Identify the relevant registrations, records and filing requirements.
  3. Review the information supplied for the relevant period.
  4. Coordinate the next compliance step and explain any outstanding information needed.
The exact requirements depend on your circumstances and applicable law. The firm should confirm eligibility, scope, fees and timelines before work begins.
