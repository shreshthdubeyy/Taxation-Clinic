Who may need this service?

Businesses, employers, proprietors and other deductors who have TDS obligations under applicable rules.

How the process may work

  1. Understand the nature of payments and deduction obligations.
  2. Review relevant deduction, deposit and challan information.
  3. Prepare the applicable statement or compliance information.
  4. Explain any corrections or follow-up steps that may be required.
