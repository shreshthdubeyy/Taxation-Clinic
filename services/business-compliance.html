What we can assist with

  • Tax authority audit representation: Professional representation, formal responses and documentation review for tax scrutiny assessments and department notices.
  • Tax problem consulting: Diagnostic review and resolution for outstanding demands, uncredited TDS, rectification under Section 154 and compliance grievances.
  • Clarification of applicable direct and indirect tax registrations for businesses.
  • Assistance identifying recurring tax compliance requirements and due dates.
  • Review of business records and documentation relevant to tax matters.

Who may need this service?

Small businesses, proprietors, professionals and other businesses seeking support with tax-related compliance.

How the process may work

  1. Discuss the business structure, activity and current requirements.
  2. Identify the applicable tax and compliance work within the firm’s scope.
  3. Review the records and information needed for that work.
  4. Agree on the next steps, responsibilities and applicable timelines.
The exact requirements depend on your circumstances and applicable law. The firm should confirm eligibility, scope, fees and timelines before work begins.
